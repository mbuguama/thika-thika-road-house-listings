Privacy Notice

How Thika House Hunter handles your information

Thika House Hunter helps renters, landlords, agents, and brokers connect around rental listings in Thika, Thika Road, and nearby areas.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, password, role, and optional phone number.
  • Landlord verification details such as phone number and national ID number when a landlord requests verification.
  • Listing details, photos, videos, prices, property type, location, and map pin information submitted by listing owners.
  • Saved homes, viewing requests, inquiries, reviews, payment records, and basic site activity needed to run the service.

How we use information

  • To create accounts, show listings, save homes, and help users contact listing owners.
  • To verify landlords, reduce fake listings, and support admin review.
  • To process listing promotions, M-Pesa payment status, and advertising placements when enabled.
  • To protect the platform from abuse and improve the rental search experience.

Your choices

You can contact the Thika House Hunter team from the contact page to ask for help with your account, listings, saved homes, or data removal. Do not share bank card details or one-time payment codes through listing messages.
