Terms

Using Thika House Hunter

These terms explain the basic rules for renters, landlords, agents, brokers, and admins using the platform.

Accounts and listings

  • Users should provide accurate account information and keep login details private.
  • Listing owners are responsible for truthful prices, photos, location pins, availability, fees, and contact details.
  • Admins may approve, reject, hide, or remove listings and accounts that appear unsafe, false, duplicated, or abusive.

Payments and promotions

  • Paid promotion and verification features may use M-Pesa when configured.
  • Thika House Hunter does not ask for card numbers, bank PINs, or one-time payment codes on registration or login pages.
  • Users should confirm property details before paying deposits or rent to any third party.

Safety

Renters should view homes safely, verify ownership or agent authority, and avoid sending sensitive documents in open messages. Report suspicious listings or accounts through the contact page.
